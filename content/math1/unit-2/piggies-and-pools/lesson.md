## Learning Focus

Represent situations with different types of growth and compare models over time.

## Big idea

- **Arithmetic / linear**: add a constant (pennies each day, gallons per minute)
- **Geometric / exponential**: multiply by a constant factor (interest, percent drain)
- Some contexts are **discrete** (whole days); others are **continuous** (time in minutes)

## Worked example

Piggy bank: start $5$ pennies, add $3$ each day $\Rightarrow f(n)=5+3n$.

Bank account: start $\$50$, grow by $3\%$ each month $\Rightarrow A(m)=50(1.03)^m$.

## Takeaways

- Domain matters: counting days vs measuring continuous time.
- Same “story family” can look linear or exponential depending on add vs multiply.
