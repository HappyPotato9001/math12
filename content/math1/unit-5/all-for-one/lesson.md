title: All for One, One for All
---

## Learning Focus

Choose and apply the best strategy (graphing, substitution, elimination) to solve systems and interpret answers in context.

## Big idea

All system strategies find the same solution; the best choice depends on the form.

- **Graphing:** quick estimate, shows number of solutions.
- **Substitution:** best when a variable is isolated.
- **Elimination:** best when coefficients match or are opposites.
- Always check in both original equations.

## Worked example

Solve $3x+2y=16$ and $5x-2y=8$.

The $y$-terms are opposites, so add: $8x=24\Rightarrow x=3$.
Then $9+2y=16\Rightarrow y=3.5$.

Check: $5(3)-2(3.5)=15-7=8$.

## Takeaways

- Match the method to the form of the equations.
- Every method gives the same ordered pair.
- Interpret the solution with units.
