title: Business Cents
---

## Learning Focus

Build systems of equations from business contexts and solve for a break-even / meeting point.

## Big idea

Two constraints → two equations. The solution $(x,y)$ must satisfy **both**.

## Worked example

Tickets: adult $a$, student $s$.

- $a+s=40$ (40 tickets)
- $12a+8s=400$ (\$400 revenue)

Solve by substitution or elimination to find how many of each.

## Takeaways

- Define variables clearly from the story.
- Graphically, the intersection is the solution.
