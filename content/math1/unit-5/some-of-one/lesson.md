---
title: Some of One, None of the Other
---

## Learning Focus

Recognize systems with no solution or infinitely many solutions by comparing slopes and intercepts.

## Big idea

Two lines in a plane can intersect once, never, or everywhere.

- **One solution:** different slopes.
- **No solution:** same slope, different $y$-intercept (parallel). Algebra gives a false statement such as $0=8$.
- **Infinitely many:** same slope and same intercept (same line). Algebra gives a true statement such as $0=0$.

## Worked example

Solve $y=2x+3$ and $y=2x-5$.

Set equal: $2x+3=2x-5 \Rightarrow 3=-5$, which is false. The lines are parallel, so there is **no solution**.

Now $2x+4y=8$ and $x+2y=4$: the first is $2$ times the second, so they are the same line and have **infinitely many** solutions.

## Takeaways

- Same slope, different intercept $\Rightarrow$ no solution.
- Same slope and intercept $\Rightarrow$ infinitely many.
- A false statement means none; a true identity means infinitely many.
