title: Pampering and Feeding Time
---

## Learning Focus

Use systems of equations to schedule time and share limited resources.

## Big idea

Scheduling and resource problems usually have two constraints: **how many** and **how much** (time, food, money).

- Let each variable stand for a quantity of one type.
- Equation 1 counts items; Equation 2 totals the resource.
- Solve by substitution or elimination and check units.

## Worked example

A groomer has $130$ minutes for $5$ pets. A bath takes $20$ min, a trim takes $30$ min. Let $b$ = baths, $t$ = trims.

- $b+t=5$
- $20b+30t=130$
- $20(5-t)+30t=130 \Rightarrow 100+10t=130 \Rightarrow t=3$, $b=2$.

Check: $40+90=130$.

## Takeaways

- One equation counts, one totals the resource.
- Keep units consistent (minutes, cups, dollars).
- Check in both equations.
