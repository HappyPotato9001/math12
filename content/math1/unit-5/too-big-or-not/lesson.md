## Learning Focus

Graph and interpret systems of linear inequalities to find all solutions that satisfy several limits at once.

## Big idea

A **system of inequalities** is satisfied by every point that makes **all** inequalities true. The solution set is the overlapping shaded region.

- Boundary line: dashed for $<,>$; solid for $\le,\ge$.
- Test a point to decide which side to shade.
- A point is a solution only if it works in every inequality.

## Worked example

Check $(1,1)$ in the system $y\ge 2x-1$ and $y<-x+6$.

- $1\ge 2(1)-1=1$ is true.
- $1<-1+6=5$ is true.

Both are true, so $(1,1)$ is in the solution region. For a limit like $x+y\le 12$, a total of $5$ brownies leaves room for at most $7$ cookies.

## Takeaways

- Solid boundary for $\le,\ge$; dashed for $<,>$.
- The solution is the overlap of all shaded regions.
- Test a point in each inequality.
