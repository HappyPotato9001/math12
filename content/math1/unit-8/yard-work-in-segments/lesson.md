title: Yard Work in Segments
---

## Learning Focus

Use coordinates to find midpoints, lengths, and endpoints of segments.

## Big idea

For endpoints $(x_1,y_1)$ and $(x_2,y_2)$:

- **Midpoint:** $\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\ \frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$
- **Distance:** $\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$
- Missing endpoint: the midpoint is the average, so $x_2=2x_m-x_1$.

## Worked example

Midpoint of $A(2,4)$ and $B(10,8)$: $\left(\frac{12}{2},\frac{12}{2}\right)=(6,6)$.

Distance from $(1,2)$ to $(7,10)$: $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10$.

If $M(4,3)$ is the midpoint and $A=(1,-1)$, then $B=(7,7)$.

## Takeaways

- Midpoint is the average of coordinates.
- Distance is the Pythagorean theorem.
- Work backward for a missing endpoint.
