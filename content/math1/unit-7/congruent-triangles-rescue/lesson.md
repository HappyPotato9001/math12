title: Congruent Triangles to the Rescue
---

## Learning Focus

Use triangle congruence and CPCTC to find unknown measures and justify claims.

## Big idea

**CPCTC** means corresponding parts of congruent triangles are congruent. The process:

- Prove triangles congruent with SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, or HL.
- Conclude that other corresponding parts are equal.
- In an isosceles triangle, base angles are equal.

## Worked example

$\triangle ABC\cong\triangle DEF$, $m\angle A=(3x+5)^\circ$, $m\angle D=50^\circ$.

By CPCTC: $3x+5=50\Rightarrow x=15$.

An isosceles triangle with a $40^\circ$ apex has base angles $\frac{180-40}{2}=70^\circ$.

## Takeaways

- Prove congruence first.
- Then use CPCTC.
- Set corresponding measures equal and solve.
