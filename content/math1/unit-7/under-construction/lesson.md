title: Under Construction
---

## Learning Focus

Use a compass and straightedge to copy segments, bisect angles and segments, and build equilateral triangles.

## Big idea

A **construction** uses only a compass and straightedge. Key facts:

- A circle is all points at the same distance from its center.
- The **perpendicular bisector** of a segment is the set of points equidistant from its endpoints.
- An **angle bisector** splits an angle into two equal angles.

## Worked example

Construct an equilateral triangle on $\overline{AB}$:

- Draw a circle centered at $A$ with radius $AB$.
- Draw a circle centered at $B$ with radius $AB$.
- Let $C$ be an intersection. Then $AC=AB=BC$.

If $AB=14$ and $M$ is its midpoint, $AM=7$. Bisecting an $80^\circ$ angle gives $40^\circ$.

## Takeaways

- Compass radius keeps lengths equal.
- Intersecting arcs locate new points.
- Justify constructions with equal radii.
