## Learning Focus

Construct perpendicular and parallel lines, inscribed hexagons, and triangle centers.

## Big idea

More constructions build on the basics.

- **Parallel line:** copy a corresponding angle at the new point.
- **Regular hexagon:** step the radius around a circle six times; side length equals the radius.
- **Circumcenter:** intersection of the perpendicular bisectors of the sides.
- **Incenter:** intersection of the angle bisectors.

## Worked example

A circle has radius $6$. Starting at a point on the circle, mark off arcs of radius $6$ around the circle. Six marks return to the start; joining them gives a regular hexagon.

The center and two adjacent vertices form an equilateral triangle, so each hexagon side is $6$.

## Takeaways

- Equal corresponding angles give parallel lines.
- Hexagon side $=$ circle radius.
- Triangle centers come from bisectors.
