---
title: Congruent Triangles
---

## Learning Focus

Use SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, and HL to decide whether triangles are congruent.

## Big idea

Triangles are congruent if these parts match:

- **SSS:** three pairs of sides.
- **SAS:** two sides and the **included** angle.
- **ASA** and **AAS:** two angles and a side.
- **HL:** hypotenuse and a leg (right triangles only).

**AAA** and **SSA** do **not** guarantee congruence.

## Worked example

Two right triangles each have hypotenuse $13$ and one leg $5$. By HL they are congruent.

The other leg: $\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=\sqrt{144}=12$ in both.

If $\triangle ABC\cong\triangle DEF$ with $AB=2x+1$ and $DE=11$, then $2x+1=11$ and $x=5$.

## Takeaways

- Mark corresponding parts.
- SAS needs the angle between the sides.
- AAA and SSA are not criteria.
