## Learning Focus

Summarize data with measures of center and spread, and choose measures that fit the data story.

## Big idea

- **Mean:** sum divided by count.
- **Median:** middle value of the ordered data.
- **IQR:** $Q_3-Q_1$, spread of the middle half.
- Outliers pull the mean but affect the median much less, so use median and IQR for skewed data.

## Worked example

Data: $2,4,6,8,10,12,14,16$.

- Median: $\frac{8+10}{2}=9$.
- $Q_1=\frac{4+6}{2}=5$, $Q_3=\frac{12+14}{2}=13$.
- IQR $=13-5=8$.

Mean of $3,5,7,9,11$ is $\frac{35}{5}=7$.

## Takeaways

- Order data before finding median and quartiles.
- Use median/IQR for skewed data.
- State the center and spread in context.
