---
title: Bridging the Gap
---

## Learning Focus

Compare two distributions using center and spread, and write conclusions in context.

## Big idea

To compare data sets, discuss **shape, center, and spread**.

- Compare means with SD, or medians with IQR.
- The same center can come with very different spread.
- Express differences in context, e.g. in multiples of the spread.

## Worked example

Class A mean is $78$; Class B mean is $71$. Difference: $78-71=7$ points.

Team A median $14$, Team B median $20$, both IQR $4$. Difference in medians is $6$, which is $\frac{6}{4}=1.5$ IQRs.

Data $10,10,10,10,10$ and $0,5,10,15,20$ both have mean $10$, but the second is far more spread out.

## Takeaways

- Match center with spread.
- Subtract centers to compare.
- Say which group is higher and by how much.
