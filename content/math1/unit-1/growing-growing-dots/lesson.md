## Learning Focus

Model a sequence with a **common ratio** using tables, graphs, and equations.

## Big idea

A **geometric sequence** multiplies by a constant ratio $r$ each step.

- Recursive: $f(n)=r\cdot f(n-1)$
- Explicit: $f(n)=f(0)\cdot r^n$

## Worked example

Start with $5$ dots and double each minute:

- $f(0)=5$, $r=2$
- Explicit: $f(t)=5\cdot 2^t$
- At $t=5$: $5\cdot 32=160$

## Takeaways

- Common ratio $\Rightarrow$ geometric sequence / exponential family.
- Explicit form uses exponents; recursive form multiplies the previous term.
