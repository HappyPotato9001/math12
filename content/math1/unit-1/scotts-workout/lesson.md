title: Scott’s Workout
---

## Learning Focus

Model a sequence with a table, graph, and explicit/recursive equations. Decide if it is arithmetic, geometric, or neither.

## Big idea

If Scott’s daily push-ups increase by the **same amount** each day, the model is arithmetic.

## Worked example

Day 1: 3 push-ups, and he adds 2 each day.

- Recursive: $f(n)=f(n-1)+2$, $f(1)=3$
- Explicit (with day $n$): $f(n)=3+2(n-1)=2n+1$
- Day 10: $2(10)+1=21$

## Takeaways

- Spot the common difference in tables and graphs (constant slope).
- Explicit forms answer “any day” questions quickly.
