title: Growing Dots
---

## Learning Focus

Describe a growing pattern with tables, graphs, and equations.

## Big idea

An **arithmetic sequence** changes by a **constant difference** $d$ each step.

- Recursive: $f(n)=f(n-1)+d$ with a start value
- Explicit: $f(n)=f(0)+dn$ (or an equivalent form with your indexing)

## Worked example

If a pattern has $4$ dots at minute $0$ and adds $3$ dots each minute:

- Recursive: $f(t)=f(t-1)+3$, $f(0)=4$
- Explicit: $f(t)=4+3t$
- At $t=100$: $f(100)=304$

## Takeaways

- Tables, graphs, and equations should tell the same story.
- Constant difference $\Rightarrow$ arithmetic sequence / linear family.
