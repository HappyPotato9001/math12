title: Elvira’s Equations
---

## Learning Focus

Solve linear equations with clear inverse-operation steps and check solutions.

## Big idea

Equations stay balanced when you do the **same operation** to both sides. Undo operations in reverse order.

## Worked example

Solve $3x-7=11$:

1. Add 7: $3x=18$
2. Divide by 3: $x=6$
3. Check: $3(6)-7=11$ ✓

## Takeaways

- Distribute and combine like terms before isolating.
- Always substitute back to verify.
