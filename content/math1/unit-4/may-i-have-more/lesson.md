---
title: May I Have More, Please?
---

## Learning Focus

Write and solve **inequalities** from real contexts, and interpret solutions.

## Big idea

Common phrases:

- "at least" $\Rightarrow\ge$; "at most" / "no more than" $\Rightarrow\le$
- "more than" $\Rightarrow>$; "fewer than" $\Rightarrow<$

Solve like an equation, except: **multiplying or dividing by a negative flips the inequality sign.**

Then interpret in context: restrict to whole numbers if you count objects.

## Worked example

You have $\$50$. Tickets cost $\$8$ plus a $\$10$ fee: $8t+10\le50$.

- $8t\le40\Rightarrow t\le5$
- At most $5$ tickets.

Another: $5x+12\le50\Rightarrow x\le7.6$. For whole items, the greatest is $7$.

Negative: $-3x+1<10\Rightarrow-3x<9\Rightarrow x>-3$ (sign flips).

## Takeaways

- Translate words to the correct symbol.
- Flip the sign when multiplying/dividing by a negative.
- Check that the answer makes sense in context (whole numbers?).
