## Learning Focus

Solve equations by **balancing**: do the same operation to both sides to keep equality.

## Big idea

An equation is like a balanced scale. Any operation applied to **both** sides keeps it balanced:

- Add or subtract the same quantity (including variable terms).
- Multiply or divide both sides by the same nonzero number.
- Distribute and combine like terms first when needed.

Goal: isolate the variable.

## Worked example

Solve $3x+5=2x+12$.

- Subtract $2x$: $x+5=12$
- Subtract $5$: $x=7$
- Check: $3(7)+5=26$ and $2(7)+12=26$ ✓

Solve $5(x-2)=3x+4$: $5x-10=3x+4\Rightarrow2x=14\Rightarrow x=7$.

## Takeaways

- Do the same thing to both sides.
- Move variable terms to one side, constants to the other.
- Always check by substituting.
