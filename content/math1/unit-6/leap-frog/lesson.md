---
title: Leap Frog
---

## Learning Focus

Compose transformations and describe a single motion that has the same result.

## Big idea

A **composition** applies one transformation, then another to the image.

- Two translations compose to a translation.
- Reflections over two **parallel** lines compose to a translation of twice the distance between the lines.
- Reflections over two **perpendicular** lines compose to a $180^\circ$ rotation about their intersection.
- Order can matter, so always apply the motions in the stated order.

## Worked example

Reflect $(a,b)$ over $x=0$, then over $x=4$.

- First: $(-a,b)$.
- Second: reflecting over $x=4$ sends $x\to 8-x$, giving $(8+a,\,b)$.

The result is a translation $8$ units right, twice the distance between the lines.

## Takeaways

- Apply transformations in order.
- Parallel mirrors give a translation.
- Perpendicular mirrors give a half-turn.
