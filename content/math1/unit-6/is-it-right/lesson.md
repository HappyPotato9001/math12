title: Is It Right?
---

## Learning Focus

Explain how rigid motions affect orientation and why right angles stay right.

## Big idea

**Orientation** is the order (clockwise or counterclockwise) of a figure's vertices.

- Translations and rotations **preserve** orientation.
- Reflections **reverse** orientation (a mirror image).
- All rigid motions preserve angle measures, so a $90^\circ$ angle stays $90^\circ$.

## Worked example

Triangle $ABC$ has sides $6,8,10$, so $6^2+8^2=10^2$ and it is a right triangle.

After any reflection, side lengths are still $6,8,10$, so the image is still a right triangle, though the vertex order now runs the opposite way.

## Takeaways

- Reflection reverses orientation.
- Right angles remain right under rigid motions.
- Side lengths unchanged $\Rightarrow$ Pythagorean relation unchanged.
