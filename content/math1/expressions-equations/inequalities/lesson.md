title: Linear Inequalities

## The idea

Inequalities compare expressions with $<$, $>$, $\le$, or $\ge$. Solving looks like solving equations — **except** when you multiply or divide by a negative number, the inequality sign flips.

## Worked example

Solve $-2x + 5 > 13$.

1. Subtract 5: $-2x > 8$
2. Divide by $-2$ and flip: $x < -4$
3. Graph: open circle at $-4$, shade left.

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting to flip the inequality when dividing by a negative.
- Using a closed circle for $<$ or $>$ (those need open circles).
- Testing a point from the wrong side of the boundary.
