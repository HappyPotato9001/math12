title: Solving Linear Equations
---

## The idea

A **linear equation** is a balance scale. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other so equality stays true. Your goal is to isolate the variable.

## Worked example

Solve $3x - 7 = 11$.

1. Add $7$ to both sides: $3x = 18$
2. Divide both sides by $3$: $x = 6$
3. Check: $3(6) - 7 = 18 - 7 = 11$. It works.

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting to flip both sides when multiplying/dividing by a negative (that matters more for inequalities).
- Combining unlike terms, like treating $3x + 2$ as $5x$.
- Skipping the check — a 10-second substitution catches most errors.
