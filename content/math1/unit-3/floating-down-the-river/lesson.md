title: Floating Down the River
---

## Learning Focus

Read and build **graphs of motion**: distance vs. time, speed as slope, and stops.

## Big idea

On a distance–time graph:

- **Slope** = speed (distance ÷ time).
- A **steeper** segment means **faster** movement.
- A **horizontal** segment means **not moving**.
- A **downward** segment means moving back toward the start.

For a segment, speed $=\dfrac{\Delta d}{\Delta t}$.

## Worked example

A raft floats $3$ mph for $2$ hours, rests for $1$ hour, then floats $2$ mph for $3$ hours.

- Segment 1: $3\cdot2=6$ mi
- Segment 2: $0$ mi (flat)
- Segment 3: $2\cdot3=6$ mi
- Total: $12$ mi in $6$ hours

A graph through $(1,4)$ and $(3,12)$ has speed $\dfrac{12-4}{3-1}=4$ mph.

## Takeaways

- Slope is speed; flat is stopped.
- Total distance is the sum over each segment.
- Graph stories should be read left to right.
