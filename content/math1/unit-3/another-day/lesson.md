## Learning Focus

**Synthesize** function features to compare linear and exponential functions and make predictions.

## Big idea

To compare two functions, line up their features:

- Starting values ($y$-intercepts)
- Rate of change (constant vs. changing)
- Intercepts, increasing/decreasing, asymptotes
- Where one **overtakes** the other (compare outputs in a table)

Exponential growth with $b>1$ eventually surpasses any linear function, even if it starts lower.

## Worked example

Compare $f(x)=2x+10$ and $g(x)=3\cdot2^x$.

| $x$ | $f$ | $g$ |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 10 | 3 |
| 2 | 14 | 12 |
| 3 | 16 | 24 |
| 4 | 18 | 48 |

$g$ starts lower but overtakes $f$ between $x=2$ and $x=3$. Both are increasing for $x\ge0$; $f$ adds $2$ each step, $g$ doubles.

## Takeaways

- Use tables to find where one function overtakes another.
- Linear: add. Exponential: multiply.
- Describe both starting values and long-term behavior.
