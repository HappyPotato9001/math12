---
title: Slope-Intercept Form
---

## The idea

The form $y = mx + b$ packs two story clues:

- $m$ is the **slope** (rate of change)
- $b$ is the **$y$-intercept** (starting value when $x = 0$)

## Worked example

A line passes through $(0, 2)$ and rises 3 units for every 1 unit right.

- Intercept $b = 2$
- Slope $m = 3$
- Equation: $y = 3x + 2$

Check another point: when $x = 2$, $y = 8$. From $(0,2)$ go right 2 and up 6 — lands on $(2,8)$.

## Common mistakes

- Mixing up $m$ and $b$.
- Computing slope as $\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta y}$ instead of $\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$.
- Forgetting a negative slope when the line falls left-to-right.
