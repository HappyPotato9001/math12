title: Vertex Form

## The idea

Vertex form is $y = a(x - h)^2 + k$. The vertex is $(h, k)$.

- If $a > 0$, the parabola opens up (minimum at the vertex).
- If $a < 0$, it opens down (maximum at the vertex).

## Worked example

Rewrite $y = x^2 - 6x + 10$ by completing the square.

1. $y = (x^2 - 6x) + 10$
2. Take half of $-6$: $-3$; square: $9$
3. $y = (x^2 - 6x + 9) + 10 - 9 = (x-3)^2 + 1$

Vertex: $(3, 1)$.

## Common mistakes

- Writing the vertex as $(-h, k)$ when the form already has $(x-h)$.
- Forgetting to balance the constant after adding the square term.
- Mixing up stretch factor $a$ with the vertex coordinates.
