---
title: Factoring Quadratics
---

## The idea

Factoring rewrites $ax^2 + bx + c$ as a product. For monic quadratics $x^2 + bx + c$, find two numbers that **add to $b$** and **multiply to $c$**.

Then the zero product property says: if $(x-p)(x-q)=0$, then $x=p$ or $x=q$.

## Worked example

Factor $x^2 + 5x + 6$.

Numbers that multiply to 6 and add to 5: $2$ and $3$.

So $x^2 + 5x + 6 = (x+2)(x+3)$.

Roots: $x=-2$ or $x=-3$.

## Common mistakes

- Mixing add/multiply targets ($b$ vs $c$).
- Dropping a common factor of $a$ when $a \\neq 1$.
- Forgetting that $(x-3)(x+3)=x^2-9$, not $x^2-6x-9$.
