title: "Transformers: Shifty y’s"
---

## Learning Focus

Describe vertical and horizontal shifts of $y=x^2$ and read the vertex from the equation.

## Big idea

$$y=(x-h)^2+k$$

- $h$ shifts **horizontally**: $x-h$ moves right $h$ (so $x+4$ moves left 4).
- $k$ shifts **vertically**: up if positive, down if negative.
- Vertex $(h,k)$; axis of symmetry $x=h$.

## Worked example

$y=(x+2)^2-5$: $h=-2$, $k=-5$. Vertex $(-2,-5)$, axis $x=-2$, opens up so the minimum value is $-5$.

## Takeaways

- Inside the square the sign is opposite to the direction of motion.
- Outside the square the sign matches the direction of motion.
- Shifts do not change the shape.
