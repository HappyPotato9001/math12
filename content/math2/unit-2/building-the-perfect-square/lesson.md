---
title: Building the Perfect Square
---

## Learning Focus

Complete the square using an area-model / perfect-square trinomial idea.

## Big idea

For $x^2+bx$, take half of $b$, square it, and add that to build $(x+\frac{b}{2})^2$.

Example: $x^2+8x$ needs $+16$ because $(\frac{8}{2})^2=16$, giving $(x+4)^2$.

## Takeaways

- Completing the square rewrites standard form into vertex form.
- Balance the equation: if you add a number, also subtract it (or add to both sides).
