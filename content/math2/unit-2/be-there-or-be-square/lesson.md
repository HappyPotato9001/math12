title: Be There or Be Square
---

## Learning Focus

Connect completing the square to the vertex, axis, and extreme value of a graph.

## Big idea

Complete the square: add and subtract $\left(\frac b2\right)^2$.

$$x^2+bx+c=\left(x+\frac b2\right)^2+c-\frac{b^2}{4}.$$

The vertex form $(x-h)^2+k$ reveals the vertex $(h,k)$ and axis $x=h$. If $a>0$, $k$ is the minimum value.

## Worked example

$y=x^2-6x+5$:

$$y=(x^2-6x+9)-9+5=(x-3)^2-4.$$

Vertex $(3,-4)$, axis $x=3$, minimum $-4$.

## Takeaways

- Half the middle coefficient, square it, add and subtract.
- Vertex form shows the graph features directly.
- Standard form shows the $y$-intercept $c$; vertex form shows the vertex.
