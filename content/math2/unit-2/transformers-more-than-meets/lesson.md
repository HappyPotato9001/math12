## Learning Focus

Describe vertical stretches, compressions, and reflections of quadratics.

## Big idea

$$y=a(x-h)^2+k$$

- $|a|>1$: vertical stretch (narrower).
- $0<|a|<1$: vertical compression (wider).
- $a<0$: reflection across the horizontal line through the vertex (opens down).

The vertex $(h,k)$ is unchanged by $a$.

## Worked example

$y=-2(x-1)^2+3$: opens down, stretched by factor 2, vertex $(1,3)$ is the maximum. At $x=2$: $y=-2(1)^2+3=1$.

## Takeaways

- Sign of $a$ gives direction; size of $|a|$ gives width.
- $a$ multiplies every $y$-distance from the vertex line.
- Combine with shifts to graph any $a(x-h)^2+k$.
