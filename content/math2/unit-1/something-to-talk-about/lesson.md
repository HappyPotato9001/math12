## Learning Focus

Model a growing pattern and recognize **quadratic** growth from tables, graphs, and recursive ideas.

## Big idea

Quadratic sequences often show:

- first differences that change
- **second differences** that are constant

Signal-bar / stacked-square patterns are classic quadratic figure sequences.

## Takeaways

- Linear: constant first differences
- Quadratic: constant second differences
- Explicit forms often look like $an^2+bn+c$
