## Learning Focus

Add, subtract, multiply, and simplify complex numbers $a+bi$.

## Big idea

- $i^2=-1$; powers cycle: $i,\,-1,\,-i,\,1$ with period 4.
- Add/subtract real and imaginary parts separately.
- Multiply by FOIL, then replace $i^2$ with $-1$.
- Conjugate of $a+bi$ is $a-bi$, and
$$(a+bi)(a-bi)=a^2+b^2.$$

## Worked example

$$(2+i)(3-i)=6-2i+3i-i^2=6+i+1=7+i.$$

## Takeaways

- Treat $i$ like a variable, then use $i^2=-1$.
- Conjugate products are real.
- For $i^n$, reduce $n$ mod 4.
