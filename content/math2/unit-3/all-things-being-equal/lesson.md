title: All Things Being Equal
---

## Learning Focus

Solve quadratic equations by graphing connections, factoring, square roots, and completing the square.

## Big idea

$x$-intercepts of $f(x)$ are solutions of $f(x)=0$.

Useful tools:

- factor + zero product property
- rewrite as $(x-h)^2=k$ and take square roots
- complete the square, then use inverse operations

## Worked example

$x^2-9x+20=0=(x-4)(x-5)$ so $x=4$ or $x=5$.

## Takeaways

- Factored form makes intercepts obvious.
- Vertex form makes square-root solving natural.
