## Learning Focus

Choose an efficient method for solving a quadratic equation.

## Big idea

- **Square roots:** form $ax^2+c=0$ (no $x$ term).
- **Factoring:** when integers multiply to $ac$ and add to $b$ easily.
- **Completing the square:** when $a=1$ and $b$ is even.
- **Quadratic formula:** always works:

$$x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}.$$

## Worked example

$3x^2-12=0$: $x^2=4$, so $x=\pm2$. Square roots is fastest.

## Takeaways

- Look at the structure before choosing a method.
- Check for a missing $b$ or $c$ term.
- The quadratic formula is the reliable fallback.
